02 / THE METHOD

Designed to be careful
where it matters most.

Entity resolution is not a search problem. It is a sequence of evidence-building decisions, tuned to favor accurate connections over aggressive coverage.

01
Normalize

Make variations comparable.

Clean punctuation, standardize common abbreviations, and preserve useful regional address signals without erasing the original record.

02
Retrieve

Build a focused candidate set.

Use complementary blocking keys to surface likely links efficiently, keeping the final inference pool small and auditable.

03
Score

Weigh independent signals.

Blend name, address, and geographic evidence into calibrated probabilities that reflect the record-level uncertainty.

04
Validate

Ship only safe outputs.

Guarantee every reference entity receives one row, every final link came from the candidate set, and every output is submission-ready.
